# Teach-Back and Handoff Guide

Use when the user wants to learn, maintain, or hand off a non-trivial change.

Explain:

1. the flow before and after the change;
2. the few most important files;
3. the main architecture decisions;
4. assumptions that were confirmed or rejected;
5. edge cases handled;
6. known edge cases not handled;
7. how to debug the feature;
8. how to rollback safely;
9. how to extend the design without breaking its contracts.

Prefer concrete explanations grounded in the actual repository.

When the goal is skill development, end with a short practical exercise or quiz based on the implementation. Do not reveal answers until the user attempts it.
